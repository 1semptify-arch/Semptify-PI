Official Wisconsin Court System links for starting, answering, and managing civil and small-claims cases. Last updated 2026-09-07.

Civil vs. Small Claims in Wisconsin
The court’s “Civil” forms category is mostly specialized actions: restraining orders, garnishments, and name changes.
For general civil cases (money over $10,000, most torts, most landlord-tenant claims above small-claims limits, etc.), Wisconsin does not provide a mandatory fill-in-the-blank complaint form. The summons must follow Wis. Stat. § 801.095 and the complaint must meet Wis. Stat. § 802.02.
Most tenant/eviction and consumer-debt cases will fall under Small Claims (Chapter 799), which has standardized forms.
